# Principles of Social Psychology – Converted to Docsify-This

> The Pressbooks book [Principles of Social Psychology – 1st International H5P Edition](https://opentextbc.ca/socialpsychology/), brought into Grav Open Publishing Space by the [Pressbooks Converter](https://pressbooks-converter.hibbittsdesign.org), then converted to plain Markdown pages for [Docsify-This](https://docsify-this.net/#/) by the [Open Publishing Course Space Bridge](https://open-publishing-course-space-bridge.hibbittsdesign.org).

View it as a website: https://docsify-this.net/?basePath=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/paulhibbitts/docsify-open-publishing-pressbooks-posp-site/main&homepage=home.md&sidebar=true&loadSidebar=_sidebar.md&loadNavbar=_navbar.md&pagination=true&searchbox=true&collapsibleSidebarGroups=true&collapseSidebarGroups=true&name=Principles%20of%20Social%20Psychology

These pages are the bridge's output as it is, shown like the book in Open Publishing Space: the home page is the book's home page, starting with its cover, title, authors and licence, then a card for each part. The sidebar starts with the book's name (a link back to its home page) and lists its chapters and pages, and Previous/Next buttons follow the pages in order. The Glossary page lists the book's terms, A to Z.

The original book is by Dr. Rajiv Jhangiani and Dr. Hammond Tarry, licensed under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Its Grav version is at https://demo.hibbittsdesign.org/variants/grav-open-publishing-quark2-pressbooks-posp – this version uses the same content:

- `home.md` – the book's home page, with its cover image beside it
- `section-1/` – Front Matter: accessibility statement, how to use this textbook, acknowledgements and more
- `section-2/` … `section-13/` – Chapters 1 to 12, each with its overview (`README.md`) and pages
- `section-14/` – Back Matter: about the authors and versioning history
- `section-15/` – the Glossary

Only the book is converted – the Grav site's other pages, such as its blog and Read Me, stay there. Images stay on Pressbooks, as in the Grav version.

Each page also keeps its Grav settings in a hidden note at the top, so the bridge can turn these pages back into the same Open Publishing Space book.
